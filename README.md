# The World in One Page — Friday 20 November 2026

One image that sums up Friday 20 November 2026 in world news: 36 stories
across six continents, with a map, three leads, an in-brief box and a
markets ticker. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 20 November's
world news in one image" bounty, $250 in USDC. See the bounty at
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

## What is in this repo

- `gen.py` — draws `entry.png` with Pillow and the DejaVu fonts. It embeds
  the story list (number, longitude, latitude, section, headline), the three
  leads and the ticker, then lays out a masthead, a lead banner, a
  schematic world map with numbered dots, three story columns and a ticker
  strip. It uses no network, no clock and no randomness.
- `entry.png` — the finished picture, 1024x1024, generated by `gen.py`.
- `main.ts` — the entry point. It copies the shipped `entry.png` to
  `/output/entry.png` (or `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set) and checks the
  copy byte for byte.
- `stories.md` — the source notes behind the 36 stories, with their
  groupings and ticker lines.

## The day in three

1. Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first three-way summit in Shenzhen and
   issue a joint peace declaration after about 70 minutes, with no
   breakthrough on Ukraine, Iran or nuclear arms.
2. The International Court of Justice rules 9-6 that Myanmar committed
   genocide against the Rohingya, the court's first such finding against
   a state.
3. US stocks fall for a fifth straight day: the S&P 500 loses 2.02% and
   the Nasdaq 2.25%, while Japan's Nikkei drops 5.1% after Anthropic's
   Nasdaq debut.

## How to rebuild the picture

```sh
python3 gen.py
```

Running it twice produces the same bytes.
